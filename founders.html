Why We Are the Perfect Team for Ziddi

Madhwesh and Gautam have been close friends for over 15 years — two athletes who spent years training together and watching talented people get injured, plateau, or quit for the same preventable reason: no one provided them with structured, intelligent guidance. Combining Madhwesh's software engineering rigor (BIT Mesra 1st Class Distinction, Fortune 1 engineering at Walmart, and a personal 30kg fitness transformation) with Gautam’s national-level athletic background, trekking endurance, and ground-level vendor partnership execution, Ziddi is built by founders who have lived every single pain point the platform solves. We don't just build features; we build the exact system we wished we had when we started out.
